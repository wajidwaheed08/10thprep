🏆 Topper Strategy

  1. Study a little every day, but never break your consistency.
  2. Understand the concept instead of only memorising answers.
  3. Whenever you get a question wrong, find out exactly where you went wrong.
  4. Revise important formulas, definitions and key points regularly.
  5. Try to solve MCQs with logic instead of guessing the answer.
  6. Identify your weak topics and give them extra practice.
  7. After every test, analyse your mistakes before starting the next one.
  8. Practise solving questions within a fixed time to improve time management.
  9. Don't practise only easy questions; challenge yourself with difficult ones too.
  10. Compete with your previous performance and keep trying to improve.
